Built around the work

The whole job, in view.

FixIO connects the steps a repair shop handles every day, so the team can see what arrived, what is waiting, and what comes next.

01 / Service tickets

Every repair has a clear next step.

Follow a device from intake and diagnosis through parts, repair, completion, and return. Keep its service number, customer, photos, work notes, and status together.

  • Search and filter the repair queue
  • Capture photos, notes, and service reports
  • See the status before the next call
02 / Parts inventory

Know what's on the shelf.

Parts have their own record: quantity, minimum stock, supplier, location, barcode, and price. Low-stock items stand out before they hold up a job.

  • Track stock changes and part use
  • Find parts by name, number, or barcode
  • Spot items below their minimum quantity
03 / Parts orders

See which parts are on the way.

A simple board follows each order from placed to shipped to received, with supplier, quantity, cost, and expected delivery close at hand.

  • Ordered, in transit, and received views
  • Supplier and tracking details in the same place
  • Receive orders into the stock workflow

Capabilities

The tools around every repair.

FixIO covers the records, paperwork and team handoffs that sit alongside the repair itself.

01

Customers & inquiries

Keep contact details, incoming requests and each customer's repair history together. Previous work is easy to find when a device returns.

02

Estimates & pricing

Build estimates from parts and services, review calculated totals, and track a quote from draft through sent, accepted or rejected.

03

Photos & documents

Attach photos, videos and documents to a repair. Add notes and mark up photos to show what was found or changed.

04

Reports & labels

Record service findings and prepare printable repair reports, shipping labels and barcode labels for parts.

05

Team & communication

Admin and technician roles separate responsibilities. When email is configured, the team can send customer messages and review the email log.

06

Optional AI assistant

With a provider configured and access enabled, the assistant can help research a repair and draft suggestions for staff to review.

Good to know

Questions about FixIO.

A few practical details about how the app fits into a repair shop.

Who is FixIO for?+

FixIO is designed around a small electronics repair team's daily work: customer inquiries, device intake, repairs, parts and orders.

Can several people work in it?+

Yes. Windows workstations can connect to a shared shop server on the local network. Admin and technician roles provide different access to the team's records and actions.

Does FixIO need an internet connection?+

The desktop app must reach its shop server to use shared records; that server can be on the same computer or local network. External services, such as cloud AI or an outside mail provider, need their own connection.

What happens when ordered parts arrive?+

Orders move through pending, shipped and received states. Receiving an order through the stock workflow can add the delivered part to inventory and record the change.

Is the AI assistant required?+

No. It is optional and requires an AI provider to be configured. If the shop uses a cloud provider, prompts and the information included in them are sent to that provider.

Can I download or buy FixIO now?+

Not yet. FixIO is currently an internal desktop project. There is no public installer or pricing page; this site shows the app and its workflow.
