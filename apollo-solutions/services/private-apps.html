Where it helps

What this service covers

Start with the people and the job the product must do.

01

Workflow first

Map the current process, its exceptions and the people responsible for each decision before defining screens or automation.

02

Access and data choices

Define who can see or change records, where data should live and which existing systems need to connect. Private does not automatically mean on-device.

03

A useful first release

Build and test the core task with the people who will use it. Document limits, backups, maintenance and later additions as part of the scope.

Project process

How we shape the work

The exact features and delivery plan are agreed after we understand your needs.

  1. 01

    Observe the work

    List the tasks, users, handoffs and data sources that matter.

  2. 02

    Agree on the first version

    Separate essential actions from later ideas and define access and success checks.

  3. 03

    Test and hand over

    Verify the workflow with representative cases and record how the app is operated and maintained.

Before a project

Useful details to bring
