What this service covers

Project process

How we shape the work

The exact features and delivery plan are agreed after we understand your needs.

  1. 01

    Map the catalogue

    Review product types, variants, content and how customers currently choose.

  2. 02

    Define the transaction

    Agree on payments, delivery, tax, stock and who handles each order stage.

  3. 03

    Build and test the journey

    Check discovery, checkout and order notifications with representative products and devices.
